The MEMS Accelerometer

Scientific Principles at Work

Core physics and engineering concepts that make non-invasive biometric tracking possible.

[01]

Inertia & Capacitance

Newton's first law causes the internal proof mass to resist sudden acceleration. This micro-deflection alters the distance $d$ between capacitor plates, changing electrical capacitance $C = \varepsilon A / d$.

[02]

3-Axis Peak Filtering

Raw acceleration vector magnitude $|a| = \sqrt{a_x^2 + a_y^2 + a_z^2}$ is evaluated against an adaptive threshold to discard noise, typing, and gestures, counting only rhythmic gait impacts.

[03]

Hemoglobin Light Absorption

Oxygenated blood has a peak absorption band in the green spectrum (~530 nm). During systolic expansion, extra blood absorbs more green photons, causing reflection dips detected by the photodiode.

[04]

Peak Interval Calculation

The time interval $\Delta t$ between consecutive PPG pulses provides the real-time cardiac period. The microprocessor computes frequency: $\text{BPM} = 60 / \Delta t$.
