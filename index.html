Feature 01 / Movement Telemetry

The MEMS Accelerometer

A fitness watch never looks at your legs. It measures the physical inertia of a microscopic proof mass suspended on silicon springs inside a microchip.

How It Works in 3 Steps
  • 1
    Inertial Lag: When your wrist moves, the proof mass resists due to inertia ($F=ma$), compressing the springs.
  • 2
    Capacitance Shift: Shifting gaps between plates change electrical capacitance ($C = \varepsilon A / d$), generating voltage.
  • 3
    Rhythm Validation: The firmware algorithm verifies periodic peaks matching human gait, ignoring erratic movements.
Feature 02 / Hemodynamic Sensing

Optical Photoplethysmography (PPG)

How green LEDs and a photodiode track blood volume fluctuations through tissue — and why green light reflection is the secret to measuring pulse.

How It Works in 3 Steps
  • 1
    Light Penetration: Dual green LEDs emit photons deep into cutaneous capillary beds.
  • 2
    Absorption vs. Reflection: In systole, blood surges $\implies$ hemoglobin absorbs more light $\implies$ less light reflects back to photodiode.
  • 3
    Frequency Calculation: The photodiode registers periodic light dips $\implies$ processor measures time between peaks ($\Delta t$) $\implies \text{BPM} = 60 / \Delta t$.
